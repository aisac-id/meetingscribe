    How MeetingScribe works

    Speech recognition. OpenAI's Whisper (tiny.en, quantized) runs in your browser via transformers.js + ONNX Runtime WASM. The model and runtime are bundled — no network calls at runtime.

    Demo. A bundled 27-second synthetic meeting (espeak voice) is transcribed with word-level timestamps so you can see the full pipeline without a microphone.

    Live. Your mic is recorded in chunks (4–15 s). Each chunk is decoded to 16 kHz mono and transcribed locally, then appended with a timestamp.

    Insights. Action items (owners, deadlines) and decisions are extracted with transparent regex rules — not a black box. Keywords are simple frequency counts.

    Limits. tiny.en is English-only and optimised for speed over accuracy; expect occasional errors on names, numbers and heavy accents. It is a note-taking aid, not a verbatim court record.
